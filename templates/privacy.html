Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, and password — along with the expense data you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services. If this ever changes — for example, to add optional integrations — we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
